# Illume marketing research

Research date: 11 October 2026. Competitor facts come from official pages. The audience and campaign proposals below are hypotheses to test, not proven demand or forecasts.

## Product basis

The repository's [README](../README.md) describes a web EPUB/PDF library with reading-progress sync, narration, and generated illustrations for book sections. It documents a free plan with 100 MB storage and 25 lifetime images, and Pro at £12.99/month with 5 GB storage and 1,000 images/month. These are repository claims; this research did not verify deployed billing, limits, or app behavior.

## Recommended positioning to test

Start with fiction readers who already have EPUBs and want to see scenes illustrated while they read. Generated scene illustrations are the clearest distinction from the offers described above. This is not a claim that no other product offers illustrations.

Test the message: "See your stories come to life. Read your ebooks with AI-generated scene illustrations and narration."

Treat fantasy, adventure and classics as initial segments, because scenes and settings make the illustration benefit easy to demonstrate. No demand data yet establishes which segment pays or returns. Readers who dislike AI artwork may reject the feature; measure reactions before choosing an entire genre as the target.

## Acquisition experiments, in priority order

1. **Show the actual reading experience.** Make short clips that start with a passage, reveal its illustration, and show the reading/narration controls. Use a licensed sample or author-approved text. Compare a "see the scene" opening with a "read and listen" opening. Give each clip a tracked link to the same sample experience. Judge repeat readers and paid customers, not views.
2. **Run a small creator pilot.** Find book creators whose audiences read the chosen genre and share ebook recommendations. Offer a hands-on demo and ask for an honest reaction before paying for distribution. Reuse the format that produces activated readers. These are proposed campaigns, not outreach already performed.
3. **Partner with indie authors.** Offer a demonstration of an author-approved chapter with generated illustrations. Ask whether their readers want that experience. StoryOrigin's official group-promotion tools demonstrate an existing author channel organised by genre and supported by newsletter/social sharing, but do not establish that Illume can join those promotions as a software vendor. Work with participating authors rather than assuming platform eligibility. [StoryOrigin group promotions](https://storyoriginapp.com/features/book-marketing-with-author-group-promotions)
4. **Create useful search pages after choosing the audience.** Examples to investigate include "illustrated ebook reader", "AI illustrations for EPUB", and "read and listen to EPUB online". Include a working demo and a real explanation of supported files. These are keyword hypotheses; this research did not measure search volume, ranking difficulty or conversion.

## Convert interest into customers

The parent agent's source and browser inspection found an animated landing preview, a signup-first primary action, and no interactive sample. It also found source claims of "12,000+ readers" and a "4.9" average rating. Substantiate or remove those claims before campaigns. The observed upgrade modal displayed image/storage allowances without a price; £12.99/month above comes from the README and needs confirmation against live checkout.

The demonstration should reach a sample scene before asking visitors to upload a file or understand a subscription. An account-free sample is a proposed product change, not a confirmed current feature. Then explain the benefit of importing their own supported ebook and the concrete Pro limits.

Do not market £12.99 as universally cheaper: ElevenReader's annual and monthly offers make that claim unsupported. Sell the illustration experience if users value it enough to pay. Before increasing paid acquisition, read actual narration/image costs and repeat-use data to establish an affordable customer acquisition cost.

Record source-tagged visits, sample starts, successful uploads, first reading sessions, first image generation, return reading on a different day, and paid conversion. For small cohorts, show the numerator and denominator rather than a percentage alone. A source that produces fewer sign-ups but more repeat readers may be more useful than a high-view campaign.

## First month

- Week 1: choose one initial reader segment; interview a small set of current users or recruited testers; confirm what makes them return; audit the path from first visit to first illustrated scene.
- Week 2: produce several real demo clips and test the two opening messages; reuse the existing classic-slideshow scripts and artifacts where useful, adding actual product footage; prepare the sample destination and campaign tracking.
- Week 3: arrange a few creator and author pilots after the owner chooses to send outreach; collect objections and observe reading behavior.
- Week 4: compare cohorts by return reading and payment; repeat the best source and change the weakest point in the conversion path.

These quantities and dates describe a proposed plan, not a prediction. There is no verified customer count, retention baseline, search-volume estimate, ad result or acquisition-cost estimate in this research.
